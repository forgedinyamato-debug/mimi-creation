Storm days become story days.

The world was very grey. A big, buzzy storm made everything quiet. Everyone was waiting for a hero.

So a little hero was made. In a cozy lab, in the middle of the storm, ZEN was created. Not with one face, but with 1,111 faces! Some silly, some sleepy, some super sparkly.

His job is simple: to turn stormy days into story days.

1,111 friends. One big heart. One mission: to create joy.

The kids comic universe.

The first NFT where your character becomes a kids hero. This is not just a PFP. This is your character in a real kids comic book: fun, colorful and kind, for kids and the kid in all of us. No scary stuff, just adventure, friendship and imagination. Think Bluey meets Spider-Verse.

ZEN wakes up in a new world each chapter and helps his new friends with his special drip and powers.
